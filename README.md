# Varyk's Kryszard and Roofoo S14 Fusion

A Project Diablo 2 Season 14 loot filter. It is [Kryszard's PD2 Loot Filter](https://github.com/Kryszard-POD/Kryszard-s-PD2-Loot-Filter) (design, styling and filter levels), updated for Season 14, with the Holy Grail tracking from [Roofoo's filter](https://github.com/RoofooEvazan/Roofoo-s-PD2-Loot-Filter) added.

All credit for the original filters goes to Kryszard and Roofoo.

## What's different from Kryszard's S13 filter

**Holy Grail alerts**
- Unidentified uniques and set items you have not discovered yet get a purple `[GRAIL]` tag, a large minimap border, a star pattern and Roofoo's grail sound.
- Rings, amulets and other bases that can roll several uniques keep saying GRAIL until you have found every possible one.
- Kryszard's GG and Uber items keep their own big display and get the `[GRAIL]` tag added.
- Items you have already found go back to Kryszard's normal display and sounds.

**Season 14 updates**
- New maps: The Poisoned Well (T18), The Fortress of Aranoch (T29), The Descent from Mount Arreat (T2A)
- New town areas included in "show everything in town"
- New uniques Spectral Plate and Warriv's Warder in the gamble and Gheed lists
- New runewords Madness, Finesse, Deception and Jealousy in base and rune tooltips

**Other changes**
- Identified boots show their total resist (for example "95R") and a Dual, Tri or Quad Res line, from Roofoo's filter.
- Filter level 6 (Strict) hides health and mana potions. Full and 35% rejuvenation potions still show.
- Filter level 7 (Strict w/o Flawless) also hides flawless gems and flawless skulls. Level 8 (Max-Strictness) hides flawless skulls too.

## Filter levels
0 Show All, 1 Hide Just Trash, 2 More Notify, 3 More Notify w/o Potions, 4 Recommended, 5 Recommended w/o Potions, 6 Strict, 7 Strict w/o Flawless, 8 Max-Strictness
